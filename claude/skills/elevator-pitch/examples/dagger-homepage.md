# Example: Dagger CI tool pitch

**Format:** product homepage
**Source:** <https://dagger.io>

---

The missing software stack for CI.

Faster machines won't fix your CI. Dagger replaces artisanal scripts with a clean API, real code, and a portable DAG execution engine. It decouples pipeline logic from its execution environment, enabling teams to trigger pipelines locally or in the cloud.

---

## Why this works

- **Hook:** "Faster machines won't fix your CI" - challenges the default assumption (throwing hardware at the problem). One sentence reframe
- **Stakes:** Implied in "artisanal scripts" - visual, slightly mocking, every engineer recognizes this pain
- **Mechanism:** "clean API, real code, and a portable DAG execution engine" - names what replaces what. "Decouples pipeline logic from execution environment" is the tech-stack-level explanation
- **Objection anticipation:** "locally or in the cloud" preempts "but I need to test CI locally"
- **CTA:** Implicit - homepage, next step is try it
- **Format lesson:** Product homepages lead with metaphor or bold reframe, then mechanism. Contrast with Ruff's factual-first README style. Both work for their context
